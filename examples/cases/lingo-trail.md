# Lingo Trail — eligibility-aware trial

Fictional language-learning app. Flutter project. The paywall appears after the first completed conversation exercise. Plus unlocks all conversation packs and offline practice. Annual subscription costs a fictional $59.99/year; a seven-day free trial applies only to eligible first-time subscribers. Existing subscribers bypass the offer.

Brand: cobalt, warm yellow, friendly but adult voice. No fabricated fluency promises, learner counts, or endorsements. Trial eligibility comes asynchronously from the existing billing wrapper and may be unknown, eligible, or ineligible.

Request: design the offer and describe how the existing screen should handle each eligibility state. Unknown eligibility must not show a guaranteed free-trial CTA. Ineligible users should see the ordinary paid offer. Do not change the billing provider. Include loading, purchase pending/cancellation, and restoration outcomes.
